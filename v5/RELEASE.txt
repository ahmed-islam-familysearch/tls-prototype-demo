Show any subset of the trees at once: every tree carries a checkbox in the scope picker and in the Trees tab, both driving one selection, with All trees and Select none at the top of each. Clicking a tree adds it to what is on screen rather than replacing your selection, and Locate now frames a tree inside the selection you already built instead of resetting it to all of them
